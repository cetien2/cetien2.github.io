Privacy Policy

Affirmations for iPhone · Effective October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Affirmations app ("the app") handles and why. The app is designed to work without an account, and the affirmations you save or write stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

Affirmations you save and affirmations you write yourself are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the app may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when the app asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (such as model, operating system version, and language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

When the app first opens, iOS asks whether you allow the app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," the advertising identifier is not shared, and you may still see ads that are not personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email, or phone number in the app.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not receive or store the affirmations you write.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
