Common questions

Where are my saved affirmations stored?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Why does the app ask about tracking?
The app is free and supported by ads. Allowing tracking lets ads be more relevant; choosing not to still lets you use everything in the app. You can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

When does the daily affirmation change?
At midnight, based on your iPhone's time zone.
